This file explains js/case3.js.
case3.js runs Case 3, the Real-World Challenge.
There are 20 messages. Each message has three steps.
Step 1 is Scam or Legitimate. That step is the official score.
Step 2 is the clue. That step is practice only.
Step 3 is the safe action. That step is practice only.
The student passes with 15 correct official answers.
Case 3 stays locked until Case 2 has been passed.

Lines 3 to 13 are variables used by the functions below.
Line 3: questions is the list case3Challenges from questions.js.
Line 4: currentQuestion is the position in that list. 0 is the first message.
Line 5: selectedAnswer is "Scam" or "Legitimate".
Line 6: selectedClue is the clue the student picked.
Line 7: selectedAction is the safe-action choice.
Line 8: score is the official score.
Line 9: clueScore is the clue practice score.
Line 10: actionScore is the safe-action practice score.
Line 11: records will hold the review for the result page.
Line 12: step is "classify", then "clue", then "action", then "explain".
Line 13: finished stops the result from being saved twice.
