This file explains js/case1.js.
case1.js runs Case 1, Email Check.
There are 20 emails. The official score is Scam or Legitimate.
The student passes with 15 correct answers.
The clue step is practice only. It does not decide the pass.

Lines 3 to 13 are variables used by the functions below.
Line 3: questions is the list case1Questions from questions.js.
Line 4: currentQuestion is the position in that list. 0 is the first email.
Line 5: selectedAnswer is "Scam" or "Legitimate" for the email on screen.
Line 6: selectedClue is the clue the student picked.
Line 7: score is the official score.
Line 8: clueScore is the practice score for clues.
Line 9: records will hold the review for the result page.
Line 10: step is "classify", then "clue", then "explain".
Line 11: finished stops the result from being saved twice.
Line 13: clueNames lists the four clue labels. The buttons on the page use these names.
